# Surface: Home (ES `/`, EN `/en/`) and service pages

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Hispanic families (incl. ITIN holders) and small-business / 1099 owners, equally weighted, mostly on phones from social media. Action: book a free 45-min consultation (Google Calendar per service) or open WhatsApp. Proof: only facts on the current site (PTIN-signed returns, bilingual, ITIN welcome, free 45-min consult, documents lists, process, FAQ, founder photo). No testimonials or figures exist; link to the Google reviews profile instead. Copy: current site's, nearly verbatim.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The free consultation is the product. The page is Louisville at blue hour, with a frosted booking console floating above the Ohio River's lights. It refuses the category default: a navy split hero, stock handshake and four equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Navy-tinted onyx canvas, graphite surfaces, ivory type, brand gold as the only chromatic action. Frosted glass only where something floats over light (nav, console, checklist, mobile action bar). Wide Archivo display at an intermediate weight, Inter body. Pill controls, 14px cards. Lion mark in ivory and gold.

STORY: The visitor sees four services, bilingual + ITIN welcome, and a real founder. They believe it is personal and serious, then book a consultation or open WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night skyline photo with river lights in the lower third and a floating glass nav pill. Centered two-line headline and subcopy. Under them, a glass console: a four-service segmented control, a gold "book free consultation" pill and WhatsApp. A facts line sits on the bottom edge. On mobile the console's CTA is visible without scrolling.

SIGNATURE: The "documents to bring" checklist ticks with a drawn gold stroke and remembers progress per service.

FORM: Pinned by the user (Mercury reference + glass + brand gold); no concept-seed roll. Seed key: none (pinned). Build path: code-led (no image generation).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
