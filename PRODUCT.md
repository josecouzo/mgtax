## Platform

## Stack

Astro (static output), plain CSS with custom properties, minimal vanilla JS. Deployed to GitHub Pages as a client demo; SEO is a second phase but the HTML must stay semantic and crawlable. Chosen by Claude after the user delegated the choice ("use whatever is best for SEO, keeping GitHub Pages in mind").

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):
- **Hispanic individuals and families in Louisville, KY** — including ITIN holders without a Social Security number — who need their federal taxes filed by someone who speaks their language and treats them with respect.
- **Small business owners and self-employed 1099 workers** — who need bookkeeping, business tax returns, LLC/Corp formation, EIN and licenses.
Many arrive from social media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) or WhatsApp on a phone. Clients outside Louisville are served 100% virtually.

## Product Purpose

MG Tax Maker LLC is a bilingual (Spanish/English) tax and financial services firm founded by Miguel Angel Marichal Garcia. The website exists to turn a visitor into a booked free 45-minute consultation (Google Calendar, one calendar per service) or a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

A single bilingual, human point of contact for the whole financial life cycle of a Hispanic family or entrepreneur — taxes, bookkeeping, credit repair and business formation — explicitly welcoming ITIN holders, where large chains offer neither the language nor the closeness.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is the job: every section moves toward a free consultation or WhatsApp.
2. Trust through clarity and a real face, never through inflated claims.
3. Spanish first, English equal — never a translated afterthought.
4. Phone-first: most visitors arrive on mobile from social media.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes people with limited English and varied digital literacy: plain language, large tap targets, high contrast on the dark theme, WCAG AA.
